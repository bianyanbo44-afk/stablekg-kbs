# Author affiliation verification

The English-language website of the Weihai campus identifies the institution as **Beijing Jiaotong University, Weihai** and uses **Weihai International College** as the college name. Its academic navigation labels the relevant programme as **Computer Science**. The institutional affiliation for the revised manuscript is:

> Weihai International College, Beijing Jiaotong University, Weihai 264401, China

The official home page was checked again on 2026-09-22. Its announcements
explicitly use "Weihai International College" and its footer gives postcode
264401. A programme name does not establish the existence of an administrative
department; the earlier inferred "Department of Computer Science" is therefore
removed. The user-provided programme, Computer Science and Technology, can be
listed separately in submission metadata when requested.
