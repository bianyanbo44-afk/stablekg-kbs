\subsection{Fixed-denominator comparison}
The historical fixed-denominator certificate is evaluated on the same unedited score. Its one-window decision differs from the renormalized certificate for 0.14\% (TeRDy, ICEWS14), 2.14\% (TeRDy, ICEWS05-15), 0.82\% (CTF, ICEWS14), 8.22\% (CTF, ICEWS05-15) of all test queries. These decisions refer to different edited-score definitions; the renormalized definition is the one used for every primary result.
