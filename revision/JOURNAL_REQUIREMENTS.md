Official guide: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/neurocomputing/publish/guide-for-authors

The guide itself presented a human-verification challenge during this session;
it was not bypassed. Google's visible index of that exact official URL states
that Neurocomputing uses **single anonymized review** and lists abstract,
keywords, and highlights among its requirements. The full current guide was

The package adopts conservative Elsevier-compatible preparation: editable
LaTeX sources; author names and affiliation included; a concise abstract under
250 words; five keywords; 3–5 highlights, each at most 85 characters; vector
scientific figures; embedded tables and figures; separate cover letter,
title-page metadata, and declarations; data/code statements; and an explicit
generative-AI disclosure immediately before the reference list. Exact online
article-type fields and upload requirements should be followed when the author
opens a live submission.
